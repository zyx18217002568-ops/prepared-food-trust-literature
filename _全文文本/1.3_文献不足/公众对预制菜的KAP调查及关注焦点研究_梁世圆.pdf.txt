       和 产 业 高 质 量 发 展 提 供 参 考。 方 法             采 用 问 卷 调 查 的 方 法 ，回 收 有 效 问 卷 574 份 ，并 结 合 3 例 预 制 菜 相 关 舆 情 事

       卖场景接受度 较 高 ，对 食 堂 和 餐 厅 接 受 度 偏 低 ；公 众 消 费 行 为 呈 现 高 渗 透 、低 频 次 特 征 ，消 费 目 的 主 要 受 年 龄 和 收
       入 影 响；关 注 焦 点 在 定 义 认 知 差 异 、行 业 共 性 问 题 、知 情 权 保 障 等 方 面 。 结 论                          建 议 从 正 视 认 知 差 异 、加 强 全 链 条
       监管、推动消费端明示、促进产业提质升级等方面协同发力，构建多元共治格局。

                   A study on KAP survey and focus of public concern regarding prepared dishes
                             LIANG Shiyuan，DONG Xiaoze，GUO Ge，CHEN Xiao，WANG Jun
           （NHC Key Laboratory of Food Safety Risk Assessment, China National Center for Food Safety Risk
                                                Assessment, Beijing 100022, China）


       Abstract：Objective         To investigate the public’s cognitive level，attitude inclination，consumption behavior，and key
       concerns regarding prepared dishes in China，and to provide a reference for policy and regulatory development，public
       education，and the high-quality development of the industry. Methods          A questionnaire survey was conducted，and 574
       valid responses were collected，which were analyzed in conjunction with three public opinion incidents related to prepared
       dishes. Results    The public’s subjective awareness rate of prepared dishes was high （85. 0%），but the objective cognitive
       consistency rate was low （59. 9%），indicating a significant cognitive bias. The public generally recognized practical
       values such as convenience and timesaving，but had insufficient trust in safety and hygiene. They mainly focused on issues
       like raw material safety. Acceptance was higher for home cooking and take-out scenarios，but lower for canteens and
       restaurants. Public consumption behavior showed characteristics of high penetration but low frequency，with consumption
       purposes mainly influenced by age and income. Public opinion focused on differences in definition cognition，common
       industry problems，and the protection of the right to know. Conclusion        It is recommended to make coordinated efforts in
       acknowledging cognitive differences， enhancing whole-chain supervision， promoting consumer-end disclosure， and
       fostering industrial upgrading，to establish a multi-stakeholder governance framework.
       Keywords：Prepared dishes；public attitudes；key concerns；food safety
